Custom MIDI Files

Place your .midi files in this folder to play your own songs in the Rhythm Game.

The game automatically detects MIDI files in this directory and reads their notes, allowing you to play along with your favorite tracks!

How to use:

Add your MIDI files to this folder.
Launch the Rhythm Game in Blender.
Select your desired track and start playing!

The add-on also includes 5 default MIDI tracks, so you can start playing right away without adding your own files.
