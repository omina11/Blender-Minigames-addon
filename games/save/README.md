This folder stores your game progress, including your best times, highest scores, and personal records.

The add-on uses these save files to keep track of your achievements between sessions, so you can always come back and try to beat your own records!

Please note:

Do not delete or modify these files unless you want to reset your progress.
Save files may be created automatically as you play.
If no save files are present, the games will generate them when needed.
Have fun beating your high scores! 🎮
